# Podcasts, blogs and newsletters

## Why this page ranks alongside the books

Ultrarunning is a **podcast-shaped** sport. A large fraction of its practical knowledge — what a
coach actually does, why a protocol changed, what went wrong at a specific race — exists only in
audio, has no transcript, and is not indexed. This is the sport's characteristic evidential problem:
the substance is real, and it is unciteable in the form it is published.

The corpus rule that follows is in `SCHEMA.md` and is worth repeating: **a podcast claim nobody has
heard is a gap, not a citation.** Listing an episode here does not ground anything. Extraction is the
work.

## Podcasts

| Podcast | Host | What it is reliable for | Interest to record |
| ------- | ---- | ----------------------- | ------------------ |
| **KoopCast** | Jason Koop | Training science, coaching method, frequent guest researchers. Consistently ranked at the top of the sport's podcast lists | Koop coaches for CTS and has written the sport's main training text — he is selling the method |
| **Some Work, All Play** | Megan Roche MD and David Roche | Weekly; training, races, studies, and a strongly stated adherence-and-enjoyment philosophy | The Roches coach and have written a book on the same thesis |
| **Freetrail** | Dylan Bowman | Athlete interviews at the top of the sport — reported guests include Courtney Dauwalter, Kilian Jornet, Jim Walmsley, Sally McRae, Pau Capell | Media business; access depends on relationships with athletes and brands |
| **Science of Ultra** | Shawn Bearden | ~150 episodes of researcher interviews. **The host has stopped posting** — treat as an archive, and a good one | Academic rather than commercial |
| **Ten Junk Miles** | — | Long-form, community, culture rather than method | Entertainment |
| **Cultra Trail Running**, **Talk Ultra**, **Singletrack** | — | Named consistently in the sport's podcast listings; not evaluated in this pass | Unassessed |

**The archive point is worth acting on.** A finished podcast with 150 researcher interviews is the
single most extractable audio resource in the sport: it is bounded, it is scientific, and nobody is
adding to it. That makes it a **finite ingest project** rather than an open-ended subscription, which
is the difference between a source and a habit.

## Sites, magazines and blogs

| Site | What it is | Reliable for | Interest |
| ---- | ---------- | ------------ | -------- |
| **iRunFar** | Bryon Powell's site; the sport's paper of record for ~20 years. Race coverage, gear, its own curated blogroll | Race results, event knowledge, community. Its "blogs I read" list is a good second-order source | Media; gear affiliate revenue |
| **Trail Runner Magazine** | The main US trail magazine | Training articles, culture, features | Media |
| **Science of Running** | Steve Magness | Physiology and training theory written by a coach who reads the literature | Sells books and coaching |
| **Uphill Athlete** | House/Johnston's organisation | The aerobic-base school, mountain athletes, ADS framing | Sells plans and coaching |
| **Evoke Endurance** | Successor organisation from the same lineage; active forums | Practitioner argument, including the vertical-training threads used in this corpus | Sells plans and coaching |
| **CTS / TrainRight** | Koop's coaching company | Ultra-specific training and nutrition articles, frequently reporting studies | Sells coaching. Several figures in this corpus reach it through here |
| **Precision Fuel & Hydration** | Fuelling and sweat-testing company | Genuinely detailed fuelling and hydration content | **Sells sweat tests and electrolytes.** Its sodium position is the counterpoint recorded in this corpus |
| **UltraSignup / Trailhead** | Race registration platform plus editorial | Race data and DNF analysis — the UTMB DNF piece used here | Platform; holds data nobody else has |
| **RealEndurance** | Ultrarunning calendar and a large trail blogroll | Finding races and finding other sources | Small independent |
| **Marathon Handbook**, **The Feed** | Content and commerce sites | Serviceable summaries; secondary throughout | Affiliate and product revenue |

## Newsletters

Substack and email newsletters have absorbed a large share of the sport's writing, including athlete-
and coach-run publications. This research pass **did not survey them properly** and the page says so
rather than listing three at random. Recorded as an open lead in the source queue.

## The rules for using any of this

1. **A figure heard or read here is `acquired`, and cites this source, not the study.** The primary
   is nearly always reachable; reaching it is what moves the status.
2. **Record the commercial interest, do not use it to dismiss.** The best fuelling content in the
   sport is published by companies selling fuel. That is a reason to check, not to ignore.
3. **Prefer the archive to the feed.** A finished series can be finished. A weekly show cannot, and
   subscribing to it is not an ingest plan.
4. **Extract or drop.** A podcast on a list nobody listens to is shelf, and this corpus is not a
   shelf.
