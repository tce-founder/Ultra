**Race** Hellbender 100, Fri 7 May 2027, Old Fort NC · 102.3 mi · ~21,500 ft · 40 h cutoff

**Plan** Week 1 begins 19 October 2026. 29 weeks.

## The one thing this plan is designed against

You have 6-7 days a week available and are currently running under 20 miles a week.
**Your constraint is not time. It is how fast tendon, bone and fascia adapt** — which is
far slower than your heart and lungs will. By February you will feel capable of much more
than your legs can structurally absorb, and that gap is where this build either survives
or ends. Every conservative choice here exists to defend it.

**If you take one rule from this document:** on a week where you feel great and have a free
afternoon, do not add the session. Bank it. The plan has down weeks for a reason and the
athlete who deletes them is the athlete who does not start in May.
