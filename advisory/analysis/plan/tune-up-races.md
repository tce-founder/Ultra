## Mount Mitchell Heartbreaker — why this and not a generic 50

Proposed as the 50-mile tune-up because it is not a generic 50-miler. It is **half of
Hellbender, from the same start line, seven weeks out.**

| | Heartbreaker 50M | Hellbender 100 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start / finish | Camp Grier, Old Fort | Camp Grier, Old Fort |
| Distance | ~50 mi | 102.3 mi |
| Gain | ~12,000 ft | ~21,500 ft |
| Vert density | ~240 ft/mi | ~210 ft/mi |
| Key terrain | Snooks Nose, Green Knob, **Mt Mitchell via Black Mountain Crest**, Bald Knob Ridge, **Heartbreak Ridge** | Mt Mitchell, the same high ridges |
| Start | 5:30 am | ~4:00 am (assumed) |
| Cutoff | 16.5 h | 40 h |

**It is denser per mile than the target race**, which is the right direction for a tune-up —
it rehearses the climbing and descending rate without the duration.

The plan already had the 50-mile tune-up on Saturday 20 March 2027. **The Heartbreaker is
Saturday 20 March 2027.** No restructuring was needed, which is luck rather than planning.

**Budgeted at 13h30** against a 16.5 h cutoff. That is deliberately not an ambitious estimate —
for a first-season runner this race is a cutoff risk, not a time trial, and the point is the
rehearsal rather than the result.

## Art Loeb — the correction

**Art Loeb is not a 50-miler.** The trail is **30.1 miles** point-to-point (one source says 31),
Davidson River near Brevard to Camp Daniel Boone, with elevation gain reported between
**8,448 and 10,000 ft** depending on source and direction — FKT lists ~9,200 ft northbound.

**There is also no organised race on it** that this research pass could find. The only event
located was an "Art Loeb Adventure Run" in spring 2022, which was a guided group run rather than
a race and does not appear to be running now.

So it cannot be the 50-mile tune-up. But it is **too good a Hellbender session to drop**:

- **~300 ft of climbing per mile** — denser than Hellbender and denser than the Heartbreaker.
- Same Blue Ridge rock, root and rhododendron; same spring weather systems.
- Genuinely exposed on the balds around Black Balsam, which makes it the **cold-kit test** for
  Hellbender's 6,684 ft summit. Doing that in February is the point, not a drawback.
- Long enough to be a real fuelling rehearsal (9–12 h) without the recovery cost of a 50-miler.

**Placed at week 18 (Sat 20 Feb), Presidents' Day weekend.** Eleven weeks before Hellbender and
four weeks before the Heartbreaker — enough room to absorb it and enough room to recover.

### Why not week 25, the peak week

It was the first instinct and it is wrong. Week 25 sits four weeks before the race and only
2.5 weeks after a 50-mile race. Stacking a 9–12 hour unsupported day into that window risks
arriving at Hellbender under-recovered, and the thing week 25 should actually be doing is
**reconnaissance of Hellbender's own course**, which Art Loeb is not. Week 25 keeps its
back-to-back on the real course, now with a night section.

### Logistics — sort these before February

- **Car shuttle at both ends.** Two cars, or a shuttle service (Leap Frog Tours is named in
  local guidance). This is the thing that most often cancels a point-to-point.
- **Direction.** Southbound from Camp Daniel Boone gets the biggest climbing done early;
  northbound from Davidson River front-loads a 12.3-mile section to Gloucester Gap. Pick one
  and recce the trailhead parking.
- **Bail points.** Gloucester Gap (mile 12.3 from Davidson River) and **FS 816 at Black
  Balsam**, both road-accessible. The plan names FS 816 as the stop-here point.
- **Water.** Piped spring at Gloucester Gap, water at Cold Springs Gap. Confirm with the
  Pisgah Ranger District — sources are hiking guides, not current conditions.
- **Winter.** Ice on the balds is normal in February. Microspikes, real wind shell, and a
  turnaround rule agreed before you start.

## Still open — the week 15 50K

**No race chosen.** Needs to be a 50K on or near **Sat 30 Jan 2027**, ideally in NC with real
climbing. This research pass did not find one and did not look hard.

What it is for: the first ultra of the build, a fuelling rehearsal at 60 g/h, and a no-time-goal
confidence builder. A flat 50K would do most of that; a hilly one would do all of it.

**If nothing suitable exists on that date**, the fallback is a self-supported 50K on a mountain
weekend — the plan already has a mountain block at week 14, and week 15 could absorb it.

## Standing caveat

Every figure on this page came from race listings and aggregators, not from the organisers'
own pages — the two Tanawha Adventures and RaceRaves pages failed to resolve from this
environment. Sources disagree with each other on dates, gain and start times, and the
disagreements are recorded above rather than resolved by picking a number.

**Confirm on the official sites before entering anything.**
