# Prediction log

> Dated predictions with a confidence and a resolution date. **A wrong prediction stays visible**, or
> the confidence on the next one means nothing.
>
> This is the register that makes the rest of the system falsifiable. Advice that is never scored
> is indistinguishable from advice that is always right.

## Empty by design

No predictions recorded.

| ID | Prediction | Confidence | Made | Resolves | Outcome | What it taught |
| -- | ---------- | ---------- | ---- | -------- | ------- | -------------- |

## What is worth predicting

Things that resolve, on a date, without argument:

- Finish time and finish/DNF for a specific race
- Whether a named training block is completed above a stated session-completion rate
- Whether a fuelling rate is tolerated at race intensity by a named date
- Whether an injury permits a named session by a named date
- Whether a recorded objection converges

## What is not

Anything resolving to "it went well". If two people could disagree about the outcome after the fact,
the prediction was not written tightly enough to be scored.

## Rules

1. Confidence is a number, stated before the outcome.
2. Resolution date is set when the prediction is made, not when it is convenient.
3. **A resolved-wrong prediction is never edited.** The lesson goes in the last column.
4. Score the log periodically. A log that is 95% correct was not predicting anything difficult.
