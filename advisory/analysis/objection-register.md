# Objection register

> Objections to the plan, theme-grouped and convergence-marked. **Objections attach to decisions,
> never to the athlete.** "The fuelling plan has never been tested at race intensity" is an
> objection; "the athlete is undisciplined" is not one, and the difference is that the first can be
> answered.
>
> An objection that recurs across three reviews without being answered has **converged** and is
> either true or is being avoided. Mark it and say which.

## Themes

`training-design` · `fuelling` · `health-gate` · `time-and-adherence` · `race-strategy` ·
`evidence-quality`

The last one is specific to this repository and earns its place: **"this recommendation rests on a
figure measured on a different population"** is the objection this whole corpus was built to make
sayable. If it never appears here, the `population` field is ceremony and the charter's working
thesis is failing its own falsification test.
